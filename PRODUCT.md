# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who use Chrome's new-tab page repeatedly during focused desktop work to search several engines, reopen recent queries, and launch frequently used sites or live information widgets with minimal friction. Narrow layouts are supported for compatibility, but the primary context is keyboard-and-pointer use on desktop.

## Product Purpose

Turn a blank new tab into a fast personal command surface. Success means search is immediately available, saved destinations remain easy to scan and rearrange, dynamic widgets stay legible, and preferences follow the user without slowing the primary workflow.

## Brand Personality

Focused, direct, and adaptable. Cyber-dark is precise, amber-neumorphic is calm and tactile, and neo-brutalism is bold and poster-like without compromising repeated daily use.

## Anti-references

Avoid full-screen saturated color, low-contrast text, mixed visual languages, oversized rounded containers, decorative background patterns, and visual changes that make familiar controls harder to recognize. Soft themes must not look toy-like; brutal themes must not use blurred shadows or gradients.

## Design Principles

- Search remains the fastest path through every visual theme.
- Personal content and real data outrank decoration.
- Theme changes alter presentation, never the user's information architecture or saved ordering.
- Familiar controls, immediate feedback, and visible state make the interface trustworthy.
- Theme-specific accents stay isolated: amber marks actions in the soft theme, while cobalt blue carries actions and coral marks compact emphasis in neo-brutalism.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast for text and controls. Preserve keyboard navigation and visible focus, provide accessible names for icon controls, never rely on color alone for state, support 200% zoom and 320px reflow, and honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.
